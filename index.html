How to install

  1. Tap Download for the app you want, then open the file from the download notification or the Files app.
  2. If Android says the browser is not allowed to install apps, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source and go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect says it does not recognise the app, choose Install anyway: the demo is not on Google Play.
  4. Open the app from the app list. Each has its own name and icon colour, and all of them can be installed together.

Good to know

  • Nothing is real. No account is created, no payment is taken and nothing leaves the device.
  • Each app keeps its own data. The four apps do not talk to each other: a sale recorded in Matcha Counter does not appear in Matcha Matcha. A phone and a tablet do not share customers or orders either.
  • Demo controls. Press and hold the logo at the top of an app to reset its data. In Matcha Matcha the same sheet plays the café's part: a visit to the counter, or moving an order on to delivered.
  • Replacing an older copy. The four apps install beside the all-in-one demo and beside each other. If Android refuses an update to one already installed, uninstall the old one first.
